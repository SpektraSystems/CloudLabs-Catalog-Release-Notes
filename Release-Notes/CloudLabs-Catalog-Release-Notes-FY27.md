* Major Update - Catalog refreshed with respect to FY27.

* The catalogue has been rebuilt against the FY27 Microsoft taxonomy of **Solution Areas** and **Conversations**. All 163 tracks have been re-mapped.

> **Note**: FY27 replaces the FY26 term *Solution Play* with *Conversation*. The three Solution Areas carry forward unchanged.

* Format changes:
- Instructor-Led tracks are now published as **Guided Labs** across the catalogue.
- The **Self-Paced** and **Applied Skills** formats have been retired as separate catalogue sections. The Applied Skills courses that were retained (AI-3025, MS-4018, MS-4021, MS-4023) now appear under MOC Courses.
- Every track page now carries a standard inclusions block covering managed environment, flexible scheduling, progress tracking and tailored content.
- Lab reference codes have been introduced in the CL-FY27-XXXX format.
- Every track page carries its Solution Area and Conversation.
